Support — Lone Isle

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Lone Isle is a single-player, offline 3D real-time strategy game. You start with a few villagers on a lone island, gather resources, build a settlement, raise an army and defend it.

Contact

For questions, bug reports or suggestions: hailubai@163.com

Frequently asked questions

How do I start playing?

Open the app and pick a mode from the main menu: the tutorial walks you through nine guided steps, the campaign contains six story lines with fixed missions, and skirmish lets you choose a civilization, map, opponent count and difficulty.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. The game runs fully offline. There is no account, no login, no online multiplayer, no ads and no in-app purchases.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, inside the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Deleting the app removes all local game data.

How do I control units?

Tap a unit to select it, or press and drag to box-select several. A single-finger drag pans the camera and a pinch zooms. Use the bottom bar to gather, train units and place buildings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. All local game data is removed with it; there is no server-side data to delete.

Is the combat graphic?

No. Battles are shown as abstract low-poly units and buildings from a top-down view, with no blood or gore.

支持 — 沧海孤岛

最后更新：2026-10-26

沧海孤岛（Lone Isle）是一款单机、离线运行的 3D 即时战略游戏。你带着几名农民登上一座孤岛，采集资源、建立村落、组建军队并守住家园。

联系方式

问题反馈、故障报告或建议：hailubai@163.com

常见问题

怎么开始玩？

打开 App，在主菜单选择模式：新手教程包含 9 个引导步骤；战役包含 6 条故事线与固定关卡；自由对战可以自选文明、地图、对手数量与强度。

需要联网吗？

不需要。游戏完全离线运行，没有账号、不用登录，也没有联机对战、广告或内购。

进度存在哪里？

保存在你设备上的 App 私有目录内，不会上传到任何服务器。卸载 App 会一并删除所有本地游戏数据。

怎么操作单位？

点选单个单位，按住拖动可框选多个单位。单指拖动平移视角，双指缩放。屏幕底部按钮用于采集资源、训练单位和建造建筑。

怎么删除数据？

卸载 App 即可。所有数据都在本地，没有服务器端数据需要删除。

战斗画面血腥吗？

不血腥。战斗以俯视视角的低多边形单位与建筑表现，没有血液或断肢。
