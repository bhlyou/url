Privacy Policy — 帝国之战 / Empire War

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Summary

「帝国之战」does not collect any personal data. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and the app makes no network requests.

What data we collect

No data is collected, transmitted, or stored on any server. The app requires no account registration and no login.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, and preferences are stored only in the app's private container on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app permanently removes all local data.

Network access

The app runs fully offline. There is no analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Privacy questions: hailubai@163.com
