Trades Work Order

Offline job records, site photos, sign-off and PDF reports — built for the trade.

Write up the work while you are still on site. No signal, no account, no paperwork to catch up on later: everything is stored on your own iPhone or iPad.

What it does

  • Work orders with customer, site address, description, field notes and status
  • Work items with hours, so the total labour adds up on its own
  • Site photos from the camera or your library, with markup drawn on top (the original is always kept)
  • Customer sign-off captured with a finger and printed on the report
  • PDF reports in two templates, plus a full ZIP backup of every report, photo and signature
  • A tab layout on iPhone and a three column workspace on iPad

What it does not do

  • No account, no login, no subscription
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers
  • No network requests — it works in airplane mode
