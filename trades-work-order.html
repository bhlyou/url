Business PDF Scan, convert, organize, sign and share PDFs — all in one local library. Turn paper pages, images, Word files, TXT files or typed text into PDFs, then merge, split, compress, trim borders, correct scans, recognize text, sign, encrypt and export. Documents stay on your own iPhone or iPad until you choose to share a copy. WHAT IT DOES - Scan documents with the camera and correct skewed pages for a cleaner result - Create PDFs from images, Word documents, TXT files or text - Recognize text in scans and images with OCR - Remove white borders around scanned pages - Merge several PDFs into one, or split a PDF into separate files - Insert, delete and manage pages to keep each document in order - Compress PDFs to create smaller files that are easier to share - Add a signature, encrypt sensitive PDFs or decrypt protected files - Convert PDF pages into images for use in other apps - Keep converted files and scans together in the local library - Share finished PDFs or images through the iOS share sheet WHAT IT DOES NOT DO - No account, no login, no subscription - No ads and no third-party trackers - Documents are not uploaded to us; tools work on the local copy in the App LINKS Support: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bhlyou/url/refs/heads/main/trades-work-order-support.html Privacy policy: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bhlyou/url/refs/heads/main/trades-work-order-privacy.html Requires iOS 17 or later.
