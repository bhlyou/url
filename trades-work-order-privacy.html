Business PDF — Privacy Policy Last updated: November 21, 2026 Business PDF (商务PDF, "the App") is a document tool that runs on your device. This policy explains what data is involved when you use the App. IN SHORT - No account is required, and there is no sign-in of any kind. - Documents you import, scan or create stay in the App's local storage unless you explicitly share or export them. - The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase for anonymous, aggregated usage statistics. There are no advertising SDKs and no trackers across other apps. - We never sell personal data. DATA ON YOUR DEVICE The App stores the following locally, inside its own sandbox or its shared App Group container: - PDFs and supported documents you import - Scans, selected images and PDFs you create - Signatures and page changes you add to documents - Recognition results and exported files produced by the tools - Your library organization and app settings - A temporary shared inbox used by the Share Extension to hand files to the App CAMERA, PHOTOS AND DOCUMENT ACCESS Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a document or capture an image for PDF conversion. Photo library access is limited to the images you explicitly select, plus saving an exported image or PDF when you ask for it. File access is limited to the documents you choose through the iOS document picker or share sheet. The App does not upload the contents of your documents to us. When you export through the iOS share sheet, you decide which app or service receives the copy. The destination's own terms and privacy policy apply to that copy. NETWORK USE PDF tools work with locally available documents. The App does not require an account or cloud synchronization. Anonymous analytics events may be sent to Google Analytics for Firebase when a connection is available. If you explicitly use a local Wi-Fi transfer feature, document data is transferred over your local network with the device or computer you choose. If you export to iCloud Drive, Mail or another service, that service processes the exported copy under its own privacy policy. ANALYTICS The App records anonymous usage events such as app launches, screens opened, PDF tools selected and exports completed. Along with these events, Google receives app version, device model, operating system version and a randomly generated app instance identifier. The App does not intentionally send document contents, file names, passwords, scanned images, signatures or recognition text to analytics. These statistics are not linked to your identity, are not used for advertising and are not used to track you across other apps or websites. Google processes this data as described in its own privacy policy. Analytics is disabled entirely in development builds. FACE ID AND APP LOCKING If you enable document protection, Face ID is used by iOS to unlock the protected area. The App does not receive or store your face data. Authentication results are provided by the operating system. BACKUPS AND DELETION Your document library lives on this device. Deleting the App deletes its local container, including imported and generated documents. iOS device backups (iCloud or Finder) may include the App's data according to your own backup settings. Keep a separate exported copy of any document that matters to you. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY The App is a productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. CHANGES We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.
