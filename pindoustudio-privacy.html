Privacy Policy — Bead Pattern Studio (拼豆图纸)

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Summary

Bead Pattern Studio collects no personal identity information and requires no account. It uses Google Analytics for Firebase only for anonymous usage statistics. There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, and no cross-app tracking, so App Tracking Transparency is not required.

What we collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, address or other personally identifying information. To understand how features are used, the app sends anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • Screen views and feature events (for example generating, saving, building, printing or exporting a pattern)
  • Events contain only aggregate values such as canvas size, color brand, color count and option switches. They never include photo content, pattern pixels or text.
  • A Firebase app instance identifier, which Apple classifies as a Device ID.

These statistics are not linked to your identity, not used for advertising, and not used for cross-app tracking.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used only when you tap the capture button to take a photo that becomes your bead pattern. Photos are picked with the system photo picker, so no photo library permission is required to choose an image. The optional "Save to Photos" action uses add-only Photos authorization to write the exported chart image to your camera roll. Your photos are never uploaded and never leave your device.

On-device processing

Color quantization, background removal (Apple Vision foreground segmentation) and chart rendering all run entirely on your device. Pattern data (color grid, bead counts, progress) and source images are stored in the app's private Documents folder. Deleting the app permanently deletes all of this local data.

Network access

The app's core features work offline. The only network traffic is the anonymous usage statistics described above, sent to Google Analytics for Firebase. There is no crash reporting SDK and no telemetry beyond these statistics. Firebase data is processed and retained under Google's privacy policy.

Your choices

You can delete any pattern inside the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all local data. Because the statistics are not linked to an identity, we cannot locate an individual user from them; this data expires according to Google Analytics retention settings.

Bead color data

The bundled color charts for MARD, Coco, Perler, Artkal, Hama and IKEA are used only to match the colors of your own photo. No third-party artwork is included.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect age information. Anonymous usage statistics are not linked to identity.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact: hailubai@163.com

隐私政策 — 拼豆图纸

最后更新：2026-11-20

概要

「拼豆图纸」不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、地址等个人身份信息，也不需要注册账号。App 使用 Google Analytics for Firebase 发送匿名使用统计，不包含广告，不使用广告标识符，也不做跨 App 追踪，因此无需 ATT 授权。

我们收集什么数据

我们不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、地址等个人身份信息。为了解功能使用情况并改进产品，App 通过 Google Analytics for Firebase 发送匿名使用统计：

  • 页面访问与功能操作事件（如生成图纸、保存、拼搭、打印、导出 PDF）
  • 事件只包含尺寸、色号品牌、用色数量、参数开关等汇总信息，不包含照片内容、图纸像素数据或文字内容
  • Firebase 生成的应用实例标识（Apple 归类为 Device ID）

这些统计不关联个人身份，不用于广告，也不用于跨 App 追踪。

相机与相册

相机仅在你点按拍照按钮时使用，用来拍摄作为图纸来源的照片。选择图片使用系统相册选择器，因此无需相册权限即可选图。可选的「保存到相册」使用「仅添加」相册权限，把导出的图纸图片写入你的相册。照片不会上传，也不会离开你的设备。

本机处理

颜色量化、去背景（Apple Vision 前景分割）与图纸渲染全部在你的设备上完成。图纸数据（颜色网格、用豆数量、拼搭进度）与原图保存在 App 的本机私有 Documents 目录。卸载 App 即会永久删除全部本地数据。

网络访问

App 核心功能可离线使用。唯一的网络请求是上述匿名使用统计，发送至 Google Analytics for Firebase。App 没有崩溃上报 SDK，也没有统计之外的遥测。Firebase 数据按 Google 的隐私政策处理与保留。

您的权利

你可以随时在 App 内删除任意图纸，卸载 App 即会删除全部本地数据。匿名使用统计不关联个人身份，我们无法据此定位到具体个人；这类数据按 Google Analytics 的保留策略自动过期。

色号数据

内置的 MARD / Coco / Perler / Artkal / Hama / IKEA 色卡仅用于匹配你自己照片的颜色，不包含任何第三方美术作品。

儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集年龄信息。匿名使用统计不关联个人身份。

联系方式

如有隐私问题，请联系：hailubai@163.com
