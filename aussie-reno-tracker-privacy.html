Privacy Policy — Aussie Reno Tracker

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Aussie Reno Tracker is an offline reference and record-keeping app for Australian owner-builders. It is designed so that your project data stays on your own devices. We do not operate a server that receives your data.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no developer-operated backend.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or postal address. We do not collect device identifiers (including IDFA or IDFV). We do not collect your location. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

What you enter stays on your device

The following information is created by you and stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Project details you type, such as project name, address, state and project type
  • Checklist progress, notes and completion dates
  • Budget entries, amounts, vendors and payment status
  • Material calculator inputs and saved calculations
  • Site photos you choose to add, and their captions and phase tags

This information is not uploaded anywhere. The app has no account system and no developer-operated server, so we cannot see your projects.

Photos

When you add a site photo, iOS shows you the system photo picker and you choose the specific images to add. The app receives only the photos you select; it does not scan or read your photo library, and it does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location permission. Selected photos are copied into the app's private storage on your device.

Purchases

The app currently has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. If paid features are introduced in a future version, purchases will be handled through Apple's in-app purchase and this policy will be updated before that version ships.

Network access

The app works fully offline. Its reference content — state permit rules, approval rules, material pricing ranges and the construction checklist — is bundled inside the app. Tapping an official authority link opens that page in your browser; from that point the destination site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal information, there is no server-side data for us to access, correct or delete. You are always in control of the data on your device: you can delete individual projects, photos and entries inside the app, and removing the app deletes all of its data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
