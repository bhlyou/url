Trades Work Order — Support Offline job records, site photos, sign-off and PDF reports for tradespeople. GET HELP Email: hailubai@163.com Please include your device model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. COMMON TOPICS Write a work order Tap + on the Work Orders tab, then pick an existing customer or switch to New to add one. Site photos Open a work order, tap Add Photos or Camera. Tap a photo to mark it up; the original is always kept. Signature Open a work order, tap Collect Signature. Signatures are optional — a job never needs one to be finished or shared. PDF report Open a work order, tap Export, then choose a template. Backup Settings, then Export All Work Orders (ZIP) packs every report, photo and signature into one file. Do this regularly: your data lives only on this device, so deleting the app deletes it too. REQUIREMENTS iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. No account, no subscription and no network connection required. See also the privacy policy: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bhlyou/url/refs/heads/main/trades-work-order-privacy.html We usually respond within a few business days.
