Privacy Policy — Gatherlight: Retro Film Camera

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Gatherlight: Retro Film Camera ("the App"; shown as 拾光相机 in Simplified Chinese) is a retro camera app that simulates classic film, instant, CCD and camcorder looks in real time. This policy explains what happens to information when the App is used. In one sentence: your photos and videos never leave your device, and the only information collected is anonymous usage statistics that help improve the App.

Summary

  • No account, no registration, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases.
  • Photos, videos and negatives stay on your device. The developer cannot access them.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are collected through Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used and to fix problems. These statistics are not linked to your identity and are not used to track you across apps or websites.
  • No advertising SDKs, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no cross-app tracking.
  • All camera, filter and rendering work runs on your device.

What the App saves (on this device only)

The App stores the following in its own private storage on the device:

  • Shots you take: the developed photo and its digital negative, plus shots you import
  • App preferences: chosen camera, ISO and shutter, accessories, date stamp, grid, shutter sound and similar settings

This data never leaves the device and is never shared. Deleting a shot in the App's darkroom, or deleting the App, removes it.

In Settings you can turn on "save to album", or tap export from the darkroom, so finished photos and videos are added to your system photo library. That copy is started by you and goes only to your own library; the App itself never uploads your photos or videos.

Anonymous usage statistics

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC, to collect anonymous information about how the App is used:

  • Events such as app launch, which screen was opened, which camera was selected, that a photo or video was captured, imported, saved or shared, and whether an action succeeded or failed
  • Technical details: App version and build, device model, iOS version, device language and approximate region derived from the network connection
  • A random app-instance identifier generated by the analytics SDK, which is reset when the App is reinstalled

These statistics are used only to understand feature usage, measure feature failures and errors, and prioritise improvements. They are not linked to your name, account, photos or any other information that identifies you, and they are not used for advertising or for tracking you across other apps or websites. The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Google processes this data on its servers under its own terms; event data is retained for a limited period (up to 14 months) and then deleted. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can stop all collection by uninstalling the App. If you have a privacy request about these statistics, contact us using the address below.

Camera, microphone and photo library

Camera access is requested only when you shoot a photo or a video; microphone access only for recording video sound. The App asks to add to your photo library when saving finished photos and videos, and asks for library access only when you import a photo to process. Captured photos, videos and negatives are stored only in the App's private folder on this device and are never uploaded. No other permissions (precise location, contacts, notifications, and so on) are requested.

Third-party components

The only third-party component is Google Firebase Analytics, used for the anonymous usage statistics described above. All cameras, filters and rendering run in Apple system frameworks (AVFoundation, Metal, Core Image) entirely on the device. There are no advertising, social or tracking SDKs.

Your rights and deleting data

Because analytics data is anonymous and not linked to your identity, the developer does not hold information that can be traced back to you. You can delete individual shots in the darkroom, or delete the App to clear all locally stored data. Photos already saved to your system photo library are managed in the Photos app.

Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience camera app. It does not collect personal information from anyone, collects only the anonymous usage statistics described above, contains no advertising, and contains no links out of the App.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact hailubai@163.com.

App Store privacy form

In App Store Connect's "App Privacy" section the App declares: no data used to track you; no data linked to you; the following data collected but not linked to you — Usage Data (product interaction) and Identifiers (device ID).

隐私政策 — 拾光相机：复古胶片CCD

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 3 日

「拾光相机：复古胶片CCD」（下称「本 App」）是一款实时模拟复古胶片、拍立得、CCD 与老式摄像机效果的相机 App。本政策说明本 App 如何处理信息。一句话：你的照片和视频永远留在本机，唯一收集的信息是用于改进 App 的匿名使用统计。

一句话总结

  • 没有账号，不用注册，不用登录，没有广告，没有内购。
  • 照片、视频和底片只留在你的设备上，开发者无法访问。
  • 通过 Google Firebase Analytics 收集匿名的使用统计，用于了解功能使用情况、排查问题；这些统计不与你的身份关联，不用于跨 App 或跨网站追踪。
  • 没有广告 SDK，不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不做跨 App 追踪。
  • 相机、滤镜与渲染全部在本机完成。

本机保存什么

本 App 只会在本机的私有目录里保存：

  • 你拍下的照片：成片与对应的数字底片，以及导入的照片
  • App 偏好设置：所选相机、ISO 与快门、配件、日期水印、网格、快门声等

这些数据不会离开设备，也不会分享给任何人。在暗房里删除照片，或删除本 App，即可清除这些数据。

你可以在设置里开启「保存到相册」，或在暗房里点导出，把成片和视频加入系统相册。这一步由你主动发起，只会写入你自己的相册；本 App 本身不会上传你的任何照片或视频。

匿名使用统计

本 App 使用 Google Firebase Analytics（由 Google LLC 提供）收集匿名信息，用来了解 App 的使用情况：

  • 事件：App 启动、打开了哪个界面、选择了哪台相机、拍摄 / 导入 / 保存 / 分享了照片或视频，以及操作是否成功
  • 技术信息：App 版本与构建号、设备型号、iOS 版本、设备语言、由网络连接推断的大致地区
  • 统计 SDK 生成的随机 App 实例标识，重装 App 后会重新生成

这些统计只用于了解功能使用情况、衡量功能失败与异常、确定改进优先级。它们不与你的姓名、账号、照片或任何可以识别你身份的信息关联，也不用于广告投放，也不用于跨 App 或跨网站追踪。本 App 不使用广告标识符（IDFA），也不申请「App 追踪透明度」授权。

上述数据由 Google 按其条款在其服务器上处理；事件数据仅保留有限期限（最长 14 个月），之后删除。Google 隐私政策：policies.google.com/privacy。

卸载本 App 即可停止全部数据收集。如对上述统计有隐私相关请求，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。

相机、麦克风与相册

只有在你拍摄照片或视频时，本 App 才会申请相机权限；录制视频声音时申请麦克风权限。保存成片到系统相册时会申请「添加到相册」权限；只有在你主动导入照片进行处理时，才会申请读取相册。拍下的照片、视频和底片只保存在本机 App 的私有目录里，绝不外传。本 App 不申请精确定位、通讯录、通知等其他任何权限。

第三方组件

本 App 唯一的第三方组件是 Google Firebase Analytics，仅用于上述匿名使用统计。全部相机、滤镜与渲染都基于苹果系统框架（AVFoundation、Metal、Core Image），完全在本机运行。本 App 不集成任何广告、社交或追踪 SDK。

你的权利与删除数据

统计数据的匿名性决定了开发者不持有可以追溯到你个人的信息。你可以随时在暗房里逐张删除照片，或者直接删除本 App 来清除全部本机数据。已存入系统相册的照片请在「照片」App 中管理。

儿童隐私

本 App 是面向普通用户的相机 App，不收集任何人的个人信息，仅收集上述匿名使用统计，不含任何广告，也不包含任何跳出 App 的链接。

变更

本政策如有变更，会更新本页顶部的日期。
