Privacy Policy — Lone Isle

Last updated: 2026-10-26

What data we collect

Lone Isle does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No name, email, phone number, or address
  • No device identifiers (IDFA / IDFV)
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data

How we use data

Everything runs entirely on your device. No data is ever uploaded to any server.

Third-party SDKs

Lone Isle integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Data storage

  • Preferences (resources, progress) are stored locally with UserDefaults / SwiftData.
  • No iCloud sync.
  • No export to any external service.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no age information.

Your rights

Because no data is collected, there is no data to access, delete, or export.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: hailubai@163.com

Changes

If this policy changes, users will be notified in the app or on this page.
