FAQ

How do I start playing?

Open the app and choose a mode from the main menu: a quick skirmish against the AI, or a paced match where you build up an island settlement from the Stone Age through the Iron Age.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. The game runs fully offline. There is no account, no login, and no online multiplayer.

Where is my progress saved?

Entirely on your device, inside the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

How do I control units?

Tap a unit to select it, drag to box-select several. A single-finger drag pans the camera; pinch to zoom. Use the buttons at the bottom of the screen to gather resources, train units, and construct buildings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. All local game data is removed with it.

怎么开始玩？

打开 App，在主菜单选择模式：可以快速和 AI 打一场遭遇战，也可以从石器时代一路经营到铁器时代。

需要联网吗？

不需要。游戏完全离线运行，没有账号、不用登录，也没有联机对战。

进度存在哪里？

全部保存在你设备上的 App 私有目录内，不会上传到任何服务器。

怎么操作单位？

点选单个单位；按住拖出选框可多选。单指拖动平移视角，双指缩放。屏幕下方按钮用于采集资源、训练单位与建造建筑。

怎么删除数据？

卸载 App 即可，所有本地游戏数据会一并删除。
