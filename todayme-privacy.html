Privacy Policy — My Today: Picture Diary

Last updated: October 10, 2026

My Today: Picture Diary ("the App") is a picture diary for young children and their families. This policy explains what happens to information when the App is used. In one sentence: the App collects no data at all.

Summary

  • No account, no registration, no sign-in.
  • No personal information is collected from anyone, children included.
  • The App makes no network requests — it works fully offline.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Diaries stay on your device. The developer cannot access them.

What the App saves (on this device only)

The App stores the following in its own private storage on the device:

  • Diary entries: mood, video, voice recordings, drawings, sticker and photo pages
  • The chosen app language (set by a parent in the Parent Center)

This data never leaves the device and is never shared. Deleting a diary in the Parent Center, or deleting the App, removes it.

A parent can also export diary files from the Parent Center through the system share sheet. That export is started by the parent and goes only to a destination the parent chooses; the App itself never uploads anything.

Camera and microphone

The App asks for camera and microphone access only when a child chooses to record a video or a voice, or to take a photo. The photos, videos and recordings are saved only in the App's private folder on this device, are never uploaded, and the App does not access the photo library. No other permissions (location, contacts, notifications, and so on) are requested.

Children's privacy

The App is designed for young children and their families. It collects no personal information from children or from anyone else, and it contains no advertising. Anything that could lead a child out of the App stays behind a parental gate: the Parent Center (export, manage or delete diaries, and language settings) and opening the system Settings app both require a parent to answer a simple arithmetic question first. The App follows children's privacy requirements such as COPPA (U.S.) and the GDPR-K provisions of the GDPR (EU).

Third-party components

The App bundles two open-source components that run entirely on the device and collect no data:

  • Lottie by Airbnb (Apache License 2.0) — the animation rendering engine
  • Mood animations from Google Noto Emoji Animation (CC BY 4.0)

There are no analytics, advertising or social SDKs.

Your rights and deleting data

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, export or delete from the developer. You can delete individual diaries in the Parent Center, or delete the App to clear all locally stored data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact hailubai@163.com.

App Store privacy form

In App Store Connect's "App Privacy" section, every data type is answered "Not Collected" (Data Not Collected).

隐私政策 — 今天的我：图画日记

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 10 日

「今天的我：图画日记」（下称「本 App」）是一款给小朋友和家庭使用的图画日记。本政策说明本 App 如何处理信息。一句话：本 App 不收集任何数据。

一句话总结

  • 没有账号，不用注册，不用登录。
  • 不收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。
  • 本 App 不发起任何网络请求，全程离线运行。
  • 没有广告、没有统计、没有追踪，也没有第三方广告 / 统计 / 追踪 SDK。
  • 日记只留在你的设备上，开发者无法访问。

本机保存什么

本 App 只会在本机的私有目录里保存：

  • 日记内容：心情、视频、录音、涂鸦、贴纸和照片
  • App 语言设置（家长在家长中心里切换）

这些数据不会离开设备，也不会分享给任何人。在家长中心里删除日记，或删除本 App，即可清除这些数据。

家长还可以在家长中心通过系统分享把日记文件导出。导出由家长主动发起，只会送到家长自己选择的位置；本 App 本身不会上传任何东西。

相机与麦克风

只有在小朋友主动录视频、录音或拍照时，本 App 才会申请相机和麦克风权限。拍下的照片、视频和录音只保存在本机 App 的私有目录里，绝不外传；本 App 不访问系统相册，也不申请定位、通讯录、通知等其他任何权限。

儿童隐私

本 App 面向低龄儿童与其家庭设计，不收集儿童及任何人的个人信息，不含任何广告。凡是可能把孩子带出 App 的入口都放在家长门后面：家长中心（导出、管理、删除日记、切换语言）以及跳转到系统设置，都需要家长先答对一道简单的加法题。本 App 符合 COPPA（美国）与 GDPR-K（欧盟）等儿童隐私要求。

第三方组件

本 App 内置两个完全在本机运行、不收集任何数据的开源组件：

  • Lottie by Airbnb（Apache License 2.0）：动画渲染引擎
  • 心情动画素材：Google Noto Emoji Animation（CC BY 4.0）

本 App 不集成任何统计、广告或社交 SDK。

你的权利与删除数据

开发者不持有你的任何数据，因此不存在向开发者申请访问、导出或删除的数据。你可以随时在家长中心里逐条删除日记，或者直接删除本 App 来清除全部本机数据。

变更

本政策如有变更，会更新本页顶部的日期。

联系方式
