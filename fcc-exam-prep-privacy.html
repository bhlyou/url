Privacy Policy — FCC Commercial Radio Prep

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Summary

FCC Commercial Radio Prep collects no personal data, contains no third-party trackers or advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests.

Data We Collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, or store any information on servers. There is no account and no login.

Data Stored On Your Device

Your study activity — answers, saved mistakes, and mock-exam history — is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset Study Data" in Settings permanently deletes it.

Network Access

The app operates fully offline. The question pool is bundled inside the app binary and is never downloaded at runtime. No analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry is performed.

Question Content

All exam questions come from official FCC public documents (docs.fcc.gov), which are U.S. Government works in the public domain under 47 CFR § 13.215 and 17 U.S.C. § 105.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
